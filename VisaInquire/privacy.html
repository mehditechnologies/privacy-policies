This Privacy Policy describes how Mehdi Technologies Pvt Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our mobile application VisaInquire - Visa Tracker and associated portal services.

1. Scope and Purpose

VisaInquire is a visa tracking and client communication system designed to allow authorized applicants to monitor the real-time status, timeline milestones, and document status of their visa applications filed with their authorized travel/visa consultancy agency.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process only the minimal information required to verify identity and render tracking services:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, passport number, and email address/phone number used to authenticate your login.
  • Visa Tracking Data: Destination country, visa category (e.g. Work, Tourist, Study), application stage, issuance history, and agency status notes.
  • Applicant Documents: Document images, submission copies, or approval notices provided directly by your licensed agency or uploaded by you.
  • Device Push Notification Token: We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens exclusively to send you push alerts when your application progresses.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Policy

Strict Privacy Guarantees:
  • We do not access continuous location, GPS, or Bluetooth.
  • We do not access contact lists, call logs, SMS, or audio recordings.
  • File access is restricted strictly to files you manually choose to upload (such as applicant identity documents).

4. How We Use and Share Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your current application status and notifications.
  • Communicating important embassy/agency status updates.
  • Preventing fraudulent access and ensuring applicant account security.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

5. Security and Data Protection

All network traffic between the mobile application, web backend, and cloud databases is encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Storage is maintained with role-based authentication and secure cloud firewalls.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your personal data and applicant account. To learn how to submit a deletion request or delete your data, please visit our dedicated Data Deletion Request Page.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not intentionally solicit or collect data from children under 13 without verifiable parental/guardian consent through an authorized visa agency.
