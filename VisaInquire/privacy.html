This Privacy Policy describes how Mehdi Technologies Pvt Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects applicant information in the VisaInquire - Visa Tracker mobile application and accompanying online services.

1. Purpose and Operational Model

VisaInquire is a client-facing status tracking application for visa applicants whose applications are filed and managed through authorized travel/visa consultancy agencies. Applicant accounts are provisioned and credentialed through their handling agency; there is no open public self-registration inside the app.

2. Types of Data Processed

We only process minimal information necessary for applicant identification and tracking:

  • Identification Details: Name, passport number, and contact email/phone supplied by your licensed agency for login authentication.
  • Visa Tracking Milestones: Country applied for, visa category, milestone progress updates, and consultant notes.
  • Applicant Documents: Copies of visa notices or submission receipts managed by your processing agency.
  • Device Push Notification Token: Device push tokens (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) strictly to alert you when your application status updates.

3. Device Permissions Policy

Strict Privacy Guarantees:
  • No Camera Access: The app does not access the device camera.
  • No Continuous Location: We do not collect GPS, background location, or Bluetooth beacons.
  • No Contact or Call Logs: We never inspect your device contacts, SMS, or private files.
  • Network Access Only: The app only requires Internet access to fetch tracking updates and push notification permissions (Android 13+) to deliver status alerts.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any advertising companies or third-party brokers. Data is accessible only to:

  • Your authorized visa processing agency handling your case.
  • Essential cloud hosting and notification infrastructure (Google Firebase) solely to transmit status updates.
  • Lawful authorities if strictly required by applicable legal court order or immigration regulations.

5. Security Standards

All network data transmissions between the application and servers are secured via modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Backend databases are safeguarded by enterprise-grade access restrictions and firewalls.

6. Right to Request Data Deletion

Applicants retain full rights to have their applicant profile and associated records permanently deleted upon request. Because accounts are agency-provisioned, requests can be sent via email or through your managing visa consultant. Complete instructions are outlined on our Data Deletion Request Page.
