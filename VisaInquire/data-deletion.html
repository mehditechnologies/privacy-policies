Data & Account Deletion Request

Application: VisaInquire - Visa Tracker (Package: com.mehditechnologies.visainquire)
Developer: Mehdi Technologies Pvt Ltd
Scope: Permanent deletion of applicant profile, uploaded documents, and associated records.

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety Policy and international privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), applicants and users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

Account Provisioning Notice:
Applicant accounts on VisaInquire are issued and provisioned through your authorized visa consulting agency (there is no open public self-registration). Consequently, account and data deletion requests are processed directly through our centralized data verification desk or through your sponsoring agency.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your records via either of the following two verified methods:

Method 1: Direct Email Deletion Request (Recommended)

Send an email from your registered email address to our data protection desk:

mehditechnologiesofficial@gmail.com (or support@mehditechnologies.com)

Please specify:

  • Your Full Name
  • Your Passport Number or Registered Email
  • The Name of your Visa Agency (if known)
Method 2: Contact Your Visa Agency Administrator

You can directly ask your handling visa consultant or agency administrator to permanently delete your client file from their active VisaInquire agency database. Once deleted by the agency, all associated cloud storage records and applicant documents are purged.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon receipt and identity verification of your deletion request, the following information is permanently erased:

  • Personal Profile: Full name, passport number, contact email, phone number, and applicant credentials.
  • Uploaded Documents: Scanned copies of passports, submission notices, and verification paperwork.
  • Device Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push notification tokens.
  • Tracking Activity Logs: In-app notification logs and applicant milestone history.

Deletion Timeline & Retention Policy

Your request will be validated and executed within 7 business days. No backups or copies are retained once deletion is confirmed, with the sole exception of statutory commercial tax or invoicing records required by law between agencies and the SaaS provider.
