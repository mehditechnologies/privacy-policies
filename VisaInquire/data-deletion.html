Data & Account Deletion Request

Application: VisaInquire - Visa Tracker (Package: com.mehditechnologies.visainquire)
Developer: Mehdi Technologies Pvt Ltd
Scope: Complete deletion of user account, applicant profile, and stored personal documents.

At Mehdi Technologies Pvt Ltd, we respect your right to control your personal information. In accordance with the Google Play Data Safety Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), applicants can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request account and data deletion through any of the following simple methods:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion

Open the VisaInquire app → Go to Settings / Profile → Select "Delete My Account & Data" → Confirm your request. Your access will be terminated immediately and data scheduled for purge.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Direct Web Request)

Send an email from your registered email address to support@mehditechnologies.com with the subject line "VisaInquire Data Deletion Request". Please include:

  • Your Full Name
  • Your Registered Email Address or Passport Number
  • The name of your visa agency (if known)
Method 3: Via Your Authorized Agency

You may also ask your authorized visa agency administrator to purge your client record from their VisaInquire Agency Dashboard.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon verification and execution of your deletion request, we permanently delete:

  • Your applicant account and authentication credentials.
  • Personal identifying records: Name, phone number, email address, passport number.
  • Uploaded applicant documents, scans, and copies.
  • Device notification tokens (FCM tokens).

Retention Period & Exceptions

Data deletion is processed within 7 business days of verification. We do not retain any copies except where required by applicable financial taxation or legal compliance mandates.
